# Prayer Fire: Google Play store listing copy

Paste these into Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.
They replace the older copy in `docs/PLAYSTORE-RELEASE.md` §3 (that file still has the previous "Pray 3x" wording).

Everything below only claims what the app does today. Two honesty notes before you paste:

- **"Offline":** the Bible text is cached on the phone the first time each translation is opened with internet. After that it reads offline. It is not shipped inside the install file. The wording below says "works offline after first load".
- **"Audio":** Read Aloud uses the phone's own text-to-speech voice, so it needs a voice installed (almost every Android phone has one). It is a computer voice, not a recording of a person. The listing says "read aloud", never "recorded".

---

## 1. App title (Play limit: 30 characters)

The title you asked for, `Prayer Fire: Prayer & Audio Bible`, is **33 characters, over the limit**. Options that fit:

| Option | Characters | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **`Prayer Fire: Audio Bible`** | 24 | **Recommended.** "Prayer Fire" already says prayer; this makes "Audio Bible" the first thing a stranger reads. |
| `Prayer Fire: Prayer & Bible` | 27 | Says both, but drops "Audio". |
| `Prayer Fire - Audio Bible` | 25 | Same as the recommended one with a dash. |
| `Prayer Fire: Pray & Hear Bible` | 30 | Exactly at the limit, reads a bit awkward. |

> If you want both words "Prayer" and "Audio Bible" in the title, the only way is to drop "Fire" (for example `Prayer & Audio Bible`, 20 characters), which loses the brand name. Not recommended.

## 3. Full description (Play limit: 4000 characters)

```
Prayer Fire is a prayer app AND an Audio Bible: set alarms to pray three times a day, then read or listen to the full Holy Bible, read aloud to you, anytime.
Hear any chapter or verse read aloud with one tap on the big green Listen button. Pause, resume or stop whenever you like.

LISTEN TO THE BIBLE
- Full Holy Bible, 66 books, in KJV, ASV and WEB
- Tap "Listen" to hear a chapter read aloud, verse by verse, using your phone's own text-to-speech voice (a male voice where your phone has one)
- Select a few verses first and Listen reads just those
- Works offline after first load
- A new Verse of the Day on the home screen: tap Read to open its chapter, or Listen to hear it

READ AND STUDY
- Jump to any verse: type "John 3:16" or search a word
- Hold a verse to copy it, highlight it in colour, bookmark it, or add it to your sermon notes
- My Study keeps your bookmarks, highlights and sermon notes, and syncs when you sign in
- Larger text option, made for easy reading

PRAY EVERY DAY
- Real prayer alarms that ring on time, even when the phone is asleep or the app is closed
- Morning, midday and night watches, or set your own times
- Start-Up Prayer: a guided 7-step prayer session
- Prayer Workshop: write your prayer points, then pray them
- 500+ prayer verses by topic (faith, healing, protection and more) to pray the Word back to God
- Fasting tracker to keep a record of your consecration

PRAY WITH OTHERS
- Prayer groups and a partner prayer wall: stand in the gap for believers around the world
- Daily wisdom and testimonies to keep your heart burning

WHY 3 TIMES A DAY?
Daniel prayed three times a day. So did David and the early Church. Prayer Fire is not a rule, it is a rhythm: small, steady watches of prayer that rebuild a life of communion with God.

Free to use. Your prayer data stays yours.

Prayer Fire: Pray 3x. Read the Word. Hear the Word.
```
